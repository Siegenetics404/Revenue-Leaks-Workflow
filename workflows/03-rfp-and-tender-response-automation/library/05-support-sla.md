# Support and Service Levels

Hosted systems are covered by a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced 5 days in advance. The support desk operates 8:00 to 18:00 Philippine Time, Monday to Friday, with 24/7 on-call cover for Priority 1 incidents.

Priority 1 (system down): response within 30 minutes and a resolution target of 4 hours. Priority 2 (major degradation): response within 2 hours and a resolution target of 1 business day. Priority 3 (minor issue): response within 1 business day. Each client receives a monthly service report.

All delivered software carries a 90-day warranty after go-live, during which defects are fixed at no cost.
