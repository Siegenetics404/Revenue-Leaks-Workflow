# Pricing and Commercial Terms

We offer two commercial models: fixed price per milestone, and time and materials. Fixed price is our default for well-scoped projects. Time and materials day rates are USD 320 for a senior engineer, USD 240 for an engineer, USD 180 for a QA engineer, and USD 280 for a project manager.

Milestones are invoiced on completion and acceptance, and payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date. Quotes are valid for 60 days. Change requests are quoted separately and need written approval before work begins. Rates are fixed for the first 12 months of a contract and then adjusted by no more than 5% per year.
