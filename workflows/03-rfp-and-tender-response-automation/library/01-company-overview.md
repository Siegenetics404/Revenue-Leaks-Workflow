# Company Overview

Kestrel Digital Solutions Inc. is a software development and IT services company founded in 2016 and headquartered in Manila, Philippines. We have 42 employees, including 28 software engineers, 4 QA engineers, 3 project managers, and 2 security specialists. Our engineers average 7 years of professional experience.

We build custom web applications, mobile apps, and system integrations for government agencies, logistics firms, and healthcare providers. Our core stack is Laravel, React, and PostgreSQL, with cloud deployments on AWS and on-premise Docker environments.

We have delivered 85 projects for 60 clients since 2016, with a client retention rate of 82%. Our working languages are English and Filipino, and we work with clients in the Philippines, the United Kingdom, and Australia.
