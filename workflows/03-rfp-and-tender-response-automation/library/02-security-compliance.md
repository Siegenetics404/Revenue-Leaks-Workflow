# Security and Compliance

Kestrel Digital Solutions has held ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for our information security management system since March 2023. The certificate covers software development, hosting operations, and support services. Surveillance audits are completed annually.

All client data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Production access requires multi-factor authentication and is limited to named engineers. Access logs are retained for 12 months.

We commission an independent penetration test of every major release and at least once per year. Critical findings are fixed within 7 days and high findings within 30 days. We comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and, for UK and EU clients, with the GDPR. A named Data Protection Officer is assigned to every engagement.
