# Selected Case Studies

## City Permit Portal, a Visayas city government (2024)

We built an online permit and appointment portal for a city government in the Visayas. The system serves 120,000 registered residents and processes an average of 1,800 applications per month. Average permit processing time fell from 9 days to 3 days. The project was delivered in 5 months for a fixed price of USD 68,000.

## Fleet Tracking Platform, Harborline Logistics (2023)

We delivered real-time GPS tracking and dispatch for a fleet of 340 trucks, built with Laravel, React, and PostgreSQL. Average dispatch time dropped by 41%. The project was delivered in 7 months for USD 112,000, and the platform has run at 99.95% availability since launch.

## Patient Scheduling System, Medisys Clinics Group (2022)

We built a scheduling and SMS reminder platform for 14 clinics. The no-show rate fell from 22% to 9%. The system integrates with the client's existing billing software through a REST API. The project was delivered in 4 months for USD 54,000.
